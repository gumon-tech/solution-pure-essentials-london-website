## what-it-is

HIFU at Pure Essentials London is available for the full face, half face and neck, and for combinations of these areas. Sessions take from 45 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the area. HIFU is also available for small and medium areas of the body.

[image: room-06]

At the free consultation you can ask how the treatment works and what it aims to do for you.

## who-it-may-suit

[image: step-2-consultation]

HIFU is an aesthetic treatment for adults aged 18 and over. At the consultation we look at your skin or your goal and recommend what suits you. Results vary from person to person.

If you have a health condition, or you are unsure whether HIFU is right for you, ask at the consultation before you book.
