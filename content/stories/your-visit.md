## the-consultation

[image: step-2-consultation]

Consultations are free. At the consultation we look at your skin or your goal and recommend what suits you. Every treatment starts with a consultation; we will not recommend a treatment that is not right for you.

If you have a skin or health condition, or you are unsure about a treatment, ask at the consultation before you book. Aesthetic treatments are for adults aged 18 and over.
