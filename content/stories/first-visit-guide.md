## what-to-message

[image: step-1-message]

Tell us what you would like to change or ask about, on WhatsApp or by email. Not sure which treatment to choose? At the consultation we look at your skin or your goal and recommend what suits you.

If you have a skin or health condition, or you are unsure about a treatment, ask before you book.

## what-to-expect

[image: step-2-consultation]

Consultations are free. Every treatment starts with a consultation; we will not recommend a treatment that is not right for you. Aesthetic treatments are for adults aged 18 and over.

[image: step-3-treatment]

Your treatment is booked at a time that suits you, 7 days a week. Results vary from person to person.

## aftercare

[image: step-4-aftercare]

Your therapist gives aftercare advice at the appointment. You can message us with any question.
