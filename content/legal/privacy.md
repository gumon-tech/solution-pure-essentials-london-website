# Privacy notice

Last updated: [date to fill on publish]

This notice explains what happens to your personal information when you use this website or
contact Pure Essentials London. It is written in plain words. If anything is unclear, email us.

## Who we are

The website is run by PURE ESSENTIALS (LONDON) LTD, a company registered in England and Wales,
company number 09500632. Our registered office is 129 Station Road, London NW4 4NJ.

We decide how your information is used, so we are the "controller" for it.

To contact us about your information, email info@pureessentialslondon.com.

## What this website collects today

This website does not ask you for any information. It has no forms and no account.

The pages of this website do not use cookies of their own, do not save information in your
browser's storage, and have no analytics or advertising tags. The fonts are stored on the
website itself, so your browser does not fetch them from anyone else.

Two things on this website come from other companies, and they are described below: the
online booking calendar from Treatwell on the Book online page, and the map from Google Maps on
the contact page. The WhatsApp, email and Instagram buttons
are ordinary links; nothing is sent to those companies until you click one.

## Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub. When you visit, your browser connects
to GitHub's servers, and GitHub receives your IP address. GitHub logs IP addresses for security
purposes. GitHub keeps those logs; we have no access to them.

## The online booking calendar (Treatwell)

The page "Book online" shows a booking calendar provided by Treatwell. It is loaded from
Treatwell's own servers as soon as you open that page, so Treatwell receives your IP address
and sets its own cookies in your browser. When we checked on 13 September 2026, Treatwell set 2
cookies, named fe20-flipper-id and growthbook_id, which last up to 2 years and which Treatwell
uses to run its own features and tests. Treatwell explains its cookies in its own cookie and
privacy notices. The calendar is not shown on any other page of this website. The "Book
online" buttons on other pages take you to that page, or straight to our page on Treatwell.

When you book through the calendar, you give your details to Treatwell under its own terms, and
Treatwell passes us the booking details we need to provide your appointment.

## The map

The contact page shows a map from Google Maps. It loads as soon as you open that page, so Google
receives your IP address and the page you are on, and handles that information under its own
privacy notice. When we checked on 13 September 2026, the map did not set any cookies in your
browser. If you use the map or open it in Google Maps, Google's own terms and cookies apply from
that point.

## When you contact us

Most people contact us by WhatsApp, by email or through Treatwell. Each of these services is run by
another company, which handles your messages under its own terms and privacy notice.

### WhatsApp

WhatsApp is run by Meta. When you message us on WhatsApp, Meta processes your message and
information about it, such as your phone number and when you sent it.

If you use a WhatsApp button on this website, the message box opens with some words already typed.
Those words name the treatment you were looking at, or the part of the site you used, and a short
reference code. This helps us answer you. You can change or delete the words before you send.

We read and keep the chat so we can answer you and arrange your appointment.

### Email

When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message.
Our email is delivered through Google's email service.

### Treatwell

When you book through Treatwell, you give your details to Treatwell under its own terms. Treatwell
passes us the booking details we need to provide your appointment.

### Instagram

The Instagram link opens our Instagram page. If you message us there, Meta handles the message under
Instagram's own terms.

### Health information in your messages

A question about a treatment can include information about your health. Please share only what we
need to answer your question and book your appointment. Detailed health questions are covered at
your consultation.

## Why we use your information, and our lawful basis

The law asks us to say why we use your information and on what legal basis.

| What we do | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Answer your enquiry and arrange a booking | So we can reply and give you the appointment you ask for | Steps you ask us to take before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us |
| Use health details you choose to send us | Only to arrange the right treatment for you | Your explicit consent, which you give by sending the details to us |

You can withdraw your consent at any time by telling us. This does not affect what we did before you
told us.

## Who else receives your information

- GitHub, which hosts this website.
- Treatwell, which provides the booking calendar on the "Book online" page and handles
  bookings made through it.
- Meta, when you use WhatsApp or Instagram.
- Google, which delivers our email and provides the map on our contact page.

GitHub, Treatwell, Meta and Google may process information outside the UK, including in the
United States. Their own privacy notices explain how they protect it.

## How long we keep your information

We keep WhatsApp chats, emails and booking records for as long as we need them to arrange and provide your appointment and to meet our legal duties, and then delete them.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the information we hold about you (access);
- ask us to correct information that is wrong (correction);
- ask us to delete your information (erasure);
- ask us to limit how we use your information (restriction);
- object to how we use your information (objection);
- ask for your information in a form you can take to someone else (portability);
- withdraw your consent, where we rely on consent.

Some rights apply only in certain situations. To use any of them, email
info@pureessentialslondon.com.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it
right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK
regulator for data protection. Its website is ico.org.uk.

## When we add cookies

We plan to add measurement to this website later. Before that happens:

- the website will ask you before it uses any advertising or analytics cookies;
- no such cookies will be set, and no such tag will load, until you choose;
- Reject will be as easy to choose as Accept;
- you will be able to change your choice at any time from a link at the bottom of every page;
- this notice will be updated to list each cookie, who sets it, why, and how long it lasts.

The Treatwell cookies on the "Book online" page are described above and are not part of this
plan.

## Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when the website or the way we handle information changes. The date at
the top shows the latest version.
